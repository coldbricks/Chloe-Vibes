Wet Floor Bass is selected initially. Loop plays continuously for up to 60 seconds;
Play once plays one excerpt. Stop audio, closing the panel, or stopping all devices
ends the test. Warehouse Strut is removed from the app; original source files are
preserved outside the active selection.

Clips are stereo 48 kHz PCM16 WAV with 3 ms endpoint fades. Source levels are
preserved, and playback interpolates to the selected endpoint's sample rate.
Groove gain affects test audio only; preset, input gate and output limits are unchanged.
